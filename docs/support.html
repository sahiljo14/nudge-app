Nudge — Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does Nudge require an internet connection?

A: No. Nudge works fully offline. All your tasks are stored on your device.

Q: How do I add a task using natural language?

A: Just type or share a message like "kal assignment submit karna hai" and Nudge will extract the task and deadline automatically.

Q: Will my tasks be deleted if I uninstall the app?

A: Yes — since everything is stored locally on your device, uninstalling will remove all data.

Q: How do I share text into Nudge?

A: In any app (WhatsApp, Gmail, etc.), select text → tap Share → choose Nudge.
