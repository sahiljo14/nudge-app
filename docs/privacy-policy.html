Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2025

App name: Nudge

Developer: Sahil (individual developer)

Contact email: agneon.labs@gmail.com

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Nudge mobile application ("Nudge", "the app", "we", "our") handles information. Nudge is built as a fully offline, privacy-first task manager for students. We have designed the app so that no personal information ever leaves your device.

Data We Collect

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not gather names, email addresses, phone numbers, device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, location data, usage analytics, or any other personally identifiable information.

Local Storage

All task data — including task titles, descriptions, deadlines, and reminder settings — is stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data never leaves your device. It is not synchronized to any cloud, server, or third party. Only the Nudge app on your device has access to this data.

No User Accounts

Nudge does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any credentials to use the app. There is no user registration, no profile, and no cloud sync of any kind.

No Analytics or Tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Crashlytics, Amplitude, or any other tracking or telemetry library. We do not track how the app is used, what features are tapped, or how often the app is opened.

No Advertising

Nudge does not contain advertisements. The app does not include any advertising SDKs, ad networks, or third-party advertising libraries. We do not show ads of any kind.

Notifications

Notifications are handled entirely on your device using the flutter_local_notifications library. Reminders are scheduled and triggered locally by the operating system. No external servers, push services, or third parties are involved in delivering your reminders.

Share Intent

The app uses Android's Share Intent system to receive text that you choose to share from other apps (for example, WhatsApp, Gmail, or your browser). When you share text into Nudge, that text is used only to pre-fill the task input on your device so the app can extract a task title and deadline. This shared text is never transmitted off your device, never sent to us, and never sent to any third party.

Third-Party Sharing

No data is shared with third parties — ever. We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any information to any third party, because no information is collected in the first place.

Permissions

Nudge does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, calendar, files, or any other sensitive permissions. The only Android permissions the app uses are:

  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — used solely to restore your scheduled local reminders after the device reboots, so your reminders continue to fire as expected.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — used solely to display local reminder notifications you have set for your own tasks.
  • VIBRATE — used solely to vibrate the device when a local reminder notification fires.

Children's Privacy

Nudge does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no information at all, it is safe to use by users of any age. The app contains no in-app purchases and no advertising.

Data Deletion

Since no data is collected on our side, there is nothing to request deletion of. All your task data lives on your device only. To delete all data created by the app, simply uninstall Nudge — uninstalling the app removes all locally stored task data.

Security

Because no personal information is transmitted or stored off-device, there is no server-side data that could be exposed. Task data on your device is protected by the standard sandboxing and security features of the Android operating system.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the app's functionality or in legal requirements. When we update this policy, we will revise the "Effective date" at the top of this page. Users will see the updated date on this page. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
