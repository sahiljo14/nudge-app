Data Deletion

How to remove all your Nudge data — instantly, at any time.

🔒

Nudge is 100% offline. There are no user accounts and no servers. All your data exists only on your device. You have complete control to delete it instantly without contacting us.

Choose a deletion method

Recommended

Uninstall the app

Completely and permanently removes all Nudge data from your device.

  1. Long-press the Nudge app icon
  2. Tap Uninstall
  3. Confirm — all data is gone instantly
Instant reset

Clear app data

Wipes all data but keeps the app installed.

  1. Go to Settings → Apps
  2. Tap Nudge
  3. Tap Storage → Clear Data
  4. Confirm — app resets to fresh state
Partial

Delete individual tasks

Remove specific tasks without losing everything.

  1. Open Nudge → Tasks tab
  2. Swipe left on any task
  3. Tap Delete to confirm
